================================================================================
1. HIGH-FIDELITY MICROSTRUCTURE FEATURES
================================================================================
We transitioned the models from chart-based estimations to real-time exchange order-flow data.

-- WebSocket CVD & OFI:
* What it is: The models now use the actual, transaction-by-transaction Cumulative Volume Delta (CVD) and Order Flow Imbalance (OFI) directly from Bybit's public trade feeds.
* Simple Benefit: Tells the bot exactly whether institutional traders are actively buying or selling, instead of guessing using price wicks.

-- Real-time Liquidation Feed:
* What it is: Subscribed to Bybit's liquidation.{symbol} WebSocket feed. The bot now logs and aggregates the dollar value of long vs. short liquidations in 1-minute intervals.
* Simple Benefit: Allows the bot to spot panic and capitulation. It can buy panic drops (short liquidations peak) and sell euphoric breakouts (long liquidations peak).

-- L2 Orderbook Depth Imbalance & Spread:
* What it is: The bot now tracks the exact size of buy and sell orders in the top 10 levels of the order book and measures the exact L1 bid-ask spread.
* Simple Benefit: Prevents the bot from opening trades directly into a massive wall of sell orders, and avoids paying high spreads.

================================================================================
2. CROSS-ASSET LEAD-LAG CORRELATION FEATURES
================================================================================
We gave the models the ability to analyze how altcoins behave relative to Bitcoin.

-- BTC/ETH Momentum Leads:
* What it is: Calculated rolling return differentials between the altcoin and BTC/ETH over 5m, 1h, and 4h windows.
* Simple Benefit: Bitcoin usually moves first, and altcoins follow a few minutes later. This allows the bot to front-run altcoin moves by entering trades before the altcoin catches up.

-- Volume Dominance Ratios:
* What it is: Added the ratio of altcoin volume relative to BTC volume.
* Simple Benefit: Helps the bot identify when capital is shifting from Bitcoin into altcoins (Altseason), selecting the best coins to trade.

================================================================================
3. MACHINE-LEARNED GATING (META-LABELING)
================================================================================
We replaced the rigid, old-school trading rules with an adaptive machine-learned gate.

-- 60% Meta-Gate:
* What it is: The bot no longer rejects trades because of static, inflexible rules (like requiring RSI to be strictly above 30). Instead, the trade is passed to a secondary Meta-Classifier model. If the classifier predicts a probability of success of 60% or higher, the trade is approved.
* Simple Benefit: Significantly increases trade frequency while maintaining high accuracy. The AI dynamically adapts to different volatility levels and market regimes, only blocking trades when the context is unsafe.
